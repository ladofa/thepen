# ThePen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

ThePen is a program for drawing on the screen. It does not collect, store or send any personal information.

## What ThePen does on your computer
 * **Mouse, pen and keyboard input** : ThePen watches mouse and pen movement on the whole screen and listens to the shortcut keys you set. This is used only to draw, to show the mouse effects and to switch modes. The input is handled in memory and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
 * **Settings** : your pens, colors and shortcut keys are saved in the Windows registry of your user account (`HKEY_CURRENT_USER\SOFTWARE\ThePen`).
 * **Your files** : ThePen reads background images and overlay scripts that you put in `Documents\ThePen`. It does not read other files.
 * **Screen** : ThePen draws over the screen. It does not capture or save what is on the screen.

## What ThePen does not do
 * It does not connect to the internet.
 * It has no account, advertisement, analytics or crash report.
 * It does not share anything with the developer or any third party.
